Recognition

UGC Approved

Centurion University is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act, 1956. All online degree programmes offered by the university are approved by UGC and are valid for higher education and employment.

NAAC A+ Accredited

The university has been accredited with an 'A+' Grade by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC), reflecting its commitment to academic excellence, infrastructure, and student outcomes.

NIRF Ranked

Centurion University has been ranked by the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF), Ministry of Education, Government of India, affirming its position among the top higher education institutions in the country.
