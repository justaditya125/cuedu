About University

Centurion University of Technology and Management is a multi-sector, private university from Odisha, India. Its main campus was previously located at Parlakhemundi in the Gajapati district, with another constituent campus situated at Jatni on the outskirts of Bhubaneswar, which is now the main campus. It was accorded the status of a university in the year 2010.

Accredited with an 'A+' Grade by NAAC, Centurion is among the youngest private universities in the country to achieve this recognition.
