Admission Policy

Guidelines, merit criteria, and registration policies for Centurion University Online

Centurion University follows a transparent and merit-based admission policy. All candidates seeking admission to online degree programmes must comply with the terms and conditions outlined below.

01

All the information regarding eligibility norms and mandatory documents required and registration is available on this website.

02

The candidate has to ensure that their education / qualifying degree has been issued from a recognized university only.

03

At the time of online registration the candidates have to scan and send all their relevant documents as mentioned in the registration process on this website.

04

The admission would be processed as per the information provided by the student and if at any stage, it is found that a candidate has furnished any wrong or misleading information, his/her candidature will be cancelled immediately. In any such case, no claim for refund of any type will be entertained.

05

The student should ensure that they satisfy the eligibility norms for the program they wish to enroll.

06

Submission of documents and payments to university is subject to eligibility criteria as per the guideline of the university.

07

The candidate must deposit the fee on the date of counseling itself failing which his/her admission shall stand cancelled.

08

The student has the options as mentioned above for selecting the fees payment plan at the time of admission only.

09

Upon receipt of complete set of a mandatory documents and applicable fees, the University enrollment team would then verify all the documents with respective fees.

10

The Provisional Number would be generated within 21 working days after receipt of the complete set of documents and applicable fees. In standard conditions the student should receive the Provisional Number within 21 working days after submission of all mandatory documents & applicable fees.

11

The admission will be treated as enrolled only after Enrollment / Registration Number has been generated by University subject to provisional or confirmed enrollment.

12

University reserves the right to change the program structure, course curriculum, eligibility norms and course conduction pattern and revise fees at any point of time.

13

The prospective student should check their eligibility before enrollment and there would be no refund of any registration or part fees payment done to the university before enrollment.
