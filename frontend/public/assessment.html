Assessment & Exams

Academic evaluation guidelines, examinations schedules, and results

Click here for December 2025 cycle Results

Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIA)

CIA is conducted online through regular objective course assessments.

  • Assignments per Course2
  • Assignment TypeObjective (MCQs)
  • Questions per Assignment15 Questions
  • Marks per Question1 Mark
  • Total Assignment Marks30 Marks (15 + 15)
  • Passing Criteria40% Min Marks
  • CIA Weightage30% of Total Grade
  • Submission DeadlineBefore Term End Exams

Term End Online Examinations

TEEs are conducted online via a secure proctored examination platform.

  • Examination ScheduleJanuary & July cycles
  • Slot BookingOnline student-selected slots
  • Scheduling Window10 Days slot booking
  • Exam Duration2 Hours per subject
  • FormatObjective (49) + Essay (3)
  • Total Exam Marks70 Marks (49 + 21)
  • Exam Weightage70% of Total Grade
  • Passing Criteria40% Min in both Exam & CIA

Backlogs & Re-appear Exams

Procedures for re-attempting failed subjects or backlogs.

  • Backlog ScheduleNext exam cycle alongside subjects
  • Scheduling RequiredNone, auto-included in registration
  • Indian Students FeeINR 500 per paper
  • International/NRI FeeUSD $10 per paper
  • Fee RefundabilityNon-transferable & Non-refundable

Result Declaration

Information regarding grade reports and certifications.

  • Result TimelineWithin 30 days from last exam
  • Declaration ModeOnline PDF score cards
  • Marksheet DeliveryHardcopy couriered post-completion
  • Degree CertificateIncluded in final courier pack

Important Examination Conditions

  • Assignment completion and passing is mandatory before student is eligible for Term End Examinations.
  • Students with outstanding fee payments or documentation discrepancies are not permitted to book exam slots.
  • Project work, internships, and laboratory practical courses follow independent evaluation rules.
  • Official examination timetables and links are released by the Office of Controller of Examinations.
