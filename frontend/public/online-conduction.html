Online Conduction

Seamlessly managing your online learning journey

Centurion University conducts all its online programmes through a robust Learning Management System (LMS). Students receive provisional registration upon admission and are granted access to the LMS within one week.

The LMS serves as the central platform for all academic activities including course materials, live sessions, assessments, and discussions.
