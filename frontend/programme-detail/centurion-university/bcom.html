Online BCom

Bachelor of Commerce

About the Course

The Online B.Com programme provides a strong foundation in commerce, accounting, finance, taxation, and business practices, preparing students for rewarding careers in the corporate, banking, financial services, and entrepreneurial sectors. The curriculum blends conceptual learning with practical applications through case studies, financial analysis, projects, and industry-oriented assignments.

What You’ll Gain:

  • Comprehensive Commerce Knowledge: Build expertise in accounting, finance, taxation, economics, business law, and auditing.
  • Practical Learning Approach: Learn through case studies, financial statement analysis, projects, and real-world business scenarios.
  • Skill Development: Develop analytical, problem-solving, financial reporting, communication, and decision-making skills.
  • Career Readiness: Gain industry-relevant knowledge with internship opportunities and placement support to launch your professional career.

Career Opportunities

B.Com graduates can pursue careers in accounting, banking, finance, taxation, auditing, insurance, investment services, and entrepreneurship. Popular roles include Accountant, Financial Analyst, Tax Consultant, Banking Executive, Audit Associate, Accounts Executive, Investment Advisor, and Business Development Executive. The programme also provides a strong foundation for higher studies and professional qualifications such as CA (Chartered Accountant), CMA (Cost and Management Accountant), CS (Company Secretary), MBA, and M.Com.
